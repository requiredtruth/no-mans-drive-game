## No Man's Drive 1.8.0: performance parts and van add-ons

**Make the van faster** — carry a part to the open hood, have a toolkit nearby, and press **E**. With empty hands, **G** at the hood takes the parts back out.
- **Bigger engines:** a tier 2 high-compression engine and a tier 3 supercharged V8 drop into the engine bay instead of the stock block.
- **Turbocharger and supercharger** for more power, and a **nitrous kit**: hold **Shift** (or **NOS** on touch) while accelerating for a burst of speed.
- **Engine heat:** the dashboard now shows the engine temperature. Push hard with no radiator or low coolant and the engine overheats, wears, and finally seizes. Fit a radiator (stock, dual-fan or armored) and top up the coolant at the hood.
- **Alternator** charges the battery faster while you drive, and a **starter motor** stops the engine sputtering on start.
- **Suspension kit** for better handling, or **leaf springs** to ride higher.
- **Lithium battery** for the battery tray: the lights drain it half as fast.
- **Sand tires** for the dirt and **solid tires** that never wear, fitted wheel by wheel.

**Kit out the outside**
- **Roof rack**, with a **cargo box**, a **roof tent** to sleep in and a **spare tire mount** on top.
- **Side panniers**, a **jerry can holder** (+40 L range), a **snorkel** for deep water, a **winch** that pulls the van out of a ditch, a **ram plow**, **door armor**, a **windshield mesh**, **mud flaps**, **underglow** and a **tow hitch**.
- The van needs a tow hitch to pull a trailer now. Vans from older saves keep theirs.

Every new part has its own 3D model and can be found in garages, car trunks and scrap piles or made at the workbench. The test depot at the start of a new game includes all of them.

If the install fails over an older version, uninstall the older version first.
