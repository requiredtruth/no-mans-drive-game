# No Man's Drive 1.15.1 — Female character and wardrobe update

- Character creation now offers only Female and Male.
- The default Female character has long straight hair and a bright pink sundress, with visible long hair strands and a flared dress mesh.
- Switching the gender selector to Female applies the long-hair pink-dress starter style. Switching to Male applies a short-hair, shirt-and-jeans starter style; all wardrobe choices remain editable afterwards.
- Existing character profiles are preserved. Previous untouched Female defaults are upgraded to the new look; a previous third gender setting is migrated to Female.
- Shared multiplayer character models and wardrobe colors still synchronize to other players.
- Original v1.15.0 multiplayer, farming, vehicles and saves remain supported.
