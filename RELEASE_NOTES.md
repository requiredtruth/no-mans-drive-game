## No Man's Drive 1.7.1: fixes from playtesting

**The van**
- **Steering wheel you can see:** the wheel and column sit higher and closer, so from the driver's seat the wheel is right in front of you. The driving readout (speed, fuel, battery) moved to the top of the screen so it no longer covers the wheel and dashboard.
- **Back doors open:** walk to the back of the van and press **E** to swing the barn doors open, then step up and walk straight into the van. Each door has a small window, and the roof ladder is on the right-hand door.
- **Two steps under the rear bumper** make it easy to climb in at the back.

**Climb anything**
- Face a vehicle, trailer, crate or wall and press **Space** (or **JUMP** on touch) to pull yourself up onto it, up to about 3.7 m. Climb the van's ladder onto the roof, or hop onto a pickup's load bed. "[Space] Climb up" shows when you look at a vehicle you can climb.

**Houses**
- **Roofs are the right way up:** pitched roofs no longer sag into a V.
- **The bookshelf no longer blocks the front door:** it now stands against the inside wall, clear of every doorway.
- **Furniture moves:** the sofa, armchair, rug, kitchen table and chairs in houses are real furniture. Press **G** with empty hands to pick them up, carry them off, and place them in your van, your camp or anywhere else. Once you've taken a piece it stays gone from the house.

**Sharper shadows**
- Higher-resolution shadow maps (8192 on desktop, 4096 on phones), four shadow cascades focused close to you, and softer filtering. No more blocky stair-step shadows.

**For testing: everything at the start**
- A sedan, a pickup truck and a box truck are parked just up the road from the van, fuelled and ready to drive.
- A new game starts with a pile of every item in the game laid out in rows next to the lot, so everything can be tried. On an existing save, use **Pause → Cheats → Lay out every item (test depot)**.

**Also**
- When you hold something, the placement ghost only shows where it can actually go. The red ghost that used to stick to walls is gone.

If the install fails over an older version, uninstall the older version first.
