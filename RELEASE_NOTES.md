## No Man's Drive 1.9.0: the quality pass

This release closes the gap to the quality bar in `NOTES.md`: how things look when you aim at them, how liquids work, and how the van, houses and everyday items look up close.

**See what you're aiming at**
- Whatever you aim at gets a **white outline**: items, furniture, cupboard doors, crates, carcasses.
- Its **name shows under the cursor**, with the litres for a fuel can, coolant bottle or canteen ("Gasoline 6.50/10.00 L").
- **Actions are listed on the right**, one per row with its key cap: "Transfer [E]", "Place [G]", "Rotate [R]", "Throw [Q]". On a phone the rows name the on-screen buttons (USE, PLACE, ROT, THROW) and sit next to the crosshair.

**Liquids in litres**
- Fuel cans, coolant bottles and jugs, canteens and the coffee flask now hold an amount: "3.81/5.00 L". Cans you find are often only part full.
- At the van's **fuel cap**, the **radiator** under the hood or the **sink**, a **transfer panel** shows both containers side by side with a level gauge each, and **Ready for transfer!** when it can go. Press **E** to pour. Whatever doesn't fit stays in the can.
- **Fuel pumps have a nozzle on a hose.** With empty hands, press **E** at a pump to lift the nozzle, carry it to the fuel cap and press **E** to fill up. The hose follows you; walk too far and the nozzle snaps back. You can also fill a fuel can on the ground with the nozzle. The fuel flap on the van swings open when you bring fuel to it.
- **New: canteens.** A 1 L canteen (empty, half and full), a 2 L field canteen and a 3 L coffee flask. Fill canteens at the van's sink and brew straight into the flask at the coffee maker. Each drink is 0.25 L.
- The hotbar shows a level gauge under every liquid container.

**Houses you open by hand**
- Kitchen cabinets, fridges, wardrobes and medicine cabinets are real cupboards now. Each door swings on its own hinge, and what's inside sits on the shelves for you to take out. There's no search menu.
- Wood-grain doors with raised panels and brass or steel handles, a hanging rail in wardrobes, a mirror on the medicine cabinet.
- Supply, feed and seed crates are slatted wooden crates with real gaps between the boards, and so is the storage crate you build.

**Up close**
- Cans, drink cans, water bottles, juice, spray paint and coolant have **printed labels** from our own made-up brands (Prairie Gold beans, Fizzwell cola, Clearspring water, Tundra coolant and more), ribbed lids, pull tabs and screw caps.
- The van interior has textured wall board, ceiling, dashboard and trim (no more flat colours), with **dirt and contact shadows** along the floor edges. Houses get the same along their skirting, and cracked tile floors in kitchens and bathrooms.
- **New first-person hand**: jointed fingers, nails, your wristwatch, and a sleeve that matches the jacket you wear. Things you carry are held bigger and closer.
- Fuel pumps have a price window, a kick panel and a holster with the nozzle.

**Somewhere to drive to**
- A **Main Objective** card at the top right lists the next stop on the road trip, its distance and your progress (9 stops, from Pinecrest Junction to Frostline Pass). A white diamond marks it on the compass.
- Each stop has a green road sign and a supply crate by the road.

**Other cars get the van's parts**
- The pickup, sedan, box truck and off-roader now take the supercharger, nitrous kit (hold **Shift** or **NOS**), suspension kit, leaf springs, sand tires, solid tires, off-road tires and alternator at the hood, as well as the turbo and ram plow they took before.

If the install fails over an older version, uninstall the older version first. Saves from 1.8 load; fuel cans in them count as full.
