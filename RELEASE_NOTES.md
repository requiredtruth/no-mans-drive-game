## No Man's Drive 1.2.0

**Vehicles & modularity**
- New drivable vehicles found abandoned along the road: a **pickup truck**, **sedan**, **box truck** and **off-roader**. Each has a smooth, detailed body with glass, lights, grille, mirrors, a licence plate and a visible interior.
- **Trailers you can hitch** to any vehicle, including the motorhome:
  - a walk-in **camper trailer** with a bed and storage,
  - a **flatbed** for hauling loose items and furniture,
  - a **fuel tanker** that refuels whatever is towing it.
- Every vehicle has repairable parts, upgrades and paint jobs. Items left in a truck bed, on a flatbed or in a camper ride along, and you can place furniture in trailers.
- The **motorhome** has a new smooth Class-C body: rounded over-cab nose, sloped hood, wheel arches, inset windows with trim, and paint-matched decal stripes.

**More places**
- Procedurally generated **houses** along the road (cottage siding, brick or logs) with porches, chimneys and furnished rooms: living room, kitchen, dining table, bedroom, bathroom, searchable bookshelves, wardrobes, fridges and medicine cabinets.
- New **cabins**, **hamlets** (three houses around a lane) and **truck stops**. Roadside locations are more frequent.

**Wildlife & ambience**
- Animals now have smooth anatomical bodies with jointed legs and fur.
- Ambience no longer loops. Dozens of randomized bird species and calls, owls, crows, woodpeckers, frogs by the water, coyotes in the desert, wind gusts, rustling leaves and distant thunder, all chosen by biome and time of day, with dawn and dusk choruses and birds answering each other.

**Visuals**
- New high-res brick, siding, wallpaper, tile and carpet textures. Desert sand is no longer overexposed.
